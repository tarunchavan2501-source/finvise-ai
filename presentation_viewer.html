PROBLEM STATEMENT & SOLUTION

Why FinVise AI?

The Problem in India

  • Discretionary UPI Leaks: Frequent micro-spends on Swiggy, Zomato, and online shopping go unmonitored.
  • No Spending Ceilings: Users lack real-time warnings when rent, groceries, or dining out exceed healthy thresholds.
  • Complex Jargon: Heavy financial language confuses young professionals, college students, and homemakers.
  • Privacy Concerns: Many financial apps require bank logins, passwords, and sensitive permissions.

The FinVise AI Solution

  • Centralized Dashboard: One clean interface for monthly salary and 6 core Indian spending categories.
  • Real-time 50-30-20 Rule Check: Instant visual meters for Needs (≤50%), Wants (≤30%), and Savings (≥20%).
  • Dynamic Health Score: Instant 0–100 score rating monthly financial health.
  • 100% Privacy by Design: Zero login needed; all calculations and data stay securely in your browser.

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

System Architecture & Engineering

1. Front-End Layer

  • Pure HTML5, CSS3 & Modern JS: Zero build dependencies for instant, reliable loading.
  • Mobile-First Design: Optimized for all mobile phones, tablets, and laptops.
  • Chart.js Visualization: Real-time bar chart with dynamic orange highlighting on the highest expense.

2. Processing Engine

  • Offline-First Rule Engine: Instant budget analysis without requiring network calls.
  • 50-30-20 Rule Validator: Real-time calculation of essential needs vs lifestyle wants.
  • Compound Interest Math: Precise future value SIP projections at 12% p.a. (Nifty 50 historical average).

3. AI & Storage Layer

  • Google Gemini API: Integrated connector for advanced natural language financial guidance.
  • Browser LocalStorage: Saves user snapshots locally without external database leaks.
  • jsPDF Generator: 1-click client-side download of monthly executive reports.

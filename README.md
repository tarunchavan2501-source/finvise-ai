# FinVise AI - Personal Finance Advisor (Made for India)

> **Live Website Link**: [https://quiet-stardust-eacb3c.netlify.app](https://quiet-stardust-eacb3c.netlify.app)  
> **Platform**: Web (Fully compatible with Mobile Phones, Tablets & Laptops)  
> **Target Audience**: Salaried Professionals, College Students, Freelancers, and Household Managers in India.

---

## Project Overview

**FinVise AI** is a clean, modern personal finance assistant designed specifically for Indian economic workflows and spending habits. It enables individuals and families to record monthly take-home salary, track expenses across essential Indian categories, evaluate their financial health against the **50-30-20 rule**, and simulate long-term compound wealth growth using automated **SIP (Systematic Investment Plans)** and emergency runway calculators.

The entire platform runs client-side with zero external setup required, while supporting an optional cloud AI integration with Google Gemini.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Ready-Made Life Situation Profiles (1-Click Load)
- **Salaried Professional (Rs. 85,000/mo)**: Regular monthly salary with fixed commitments (rent, EMI), daily UPI transactions, and disciplined mutual fund investment focus.
- **College Student (Rs. 15,000/mo)**: Tight pocket allowance budgeting, mess vs eating out tracking, and early micro-investing habits (Rs. 500 SIP).
- **Freelancer (Rs. 60,000/mo)**: Uneven monthly invoices, 6-month buffer runway planning, and advance tax reserve discipline.
- **Household Manager (Rs. 1,20,000/mo)**: Combined family budget covering school fees, utilities, medical safety nets, and family goals.

### 2. Live Financial Health & 50-30-20 Rule Engine
- **Needs (Target <= 50%)**: Rent, utilities, groceries, transport.
- **Wants (Target <= 30%)**: Outings, shopping, dining out.
- **Savings (Target >= 20%)**: Automated SIP, liquid funds, emergency cushion.
- **Health Score**: Real-time evaluation out of 100 with actionable feedback.

### 3. SIP & Compound Interest Wealth Simulator
- Interactive investment slider projecting compound returns over 1, 3, 5, and 10 years at historical Nifty 50 averages (12% p.a.).
- Automated **6-Month Emergency Runway** safety calculation.

### 4. Zero Data Sharing & Privacy by Design
- No logins or credit card data required. All records and snapshots remain strictly inside the user's browser local storage.
